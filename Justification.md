# WHAT do you mean justification dawg
I have a very strong desire to avoid the problem some modpacks, particularly those of the kitchen sink variety, have where they will just throw in random mods for the sake of it rather than having the mod be there for a particular reason

As such, this document lists every single mod in the pack and describes why its present. I'll update it as I remember it exists probably\
